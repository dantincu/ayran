# A list of free apis

## No API Key Required (Truly Free Forever)

- **Open-Meteo** --- Unlimited, open-source
- **Open Notify** --- ISS position, astronauts in space
- **USGS Earthquake API** --- Real-time earthquake data
- **CoinGecko** --- Basic crypto data (rate limited but free forever)
- **PokeAPI** --- Pokemon data, no limits
- **RestCountries** --- Country data, no limits
- **OpenStreetMap/Nominatim** --- Geocoding (fair use policy)
- **Wikipedia/Wikimedia API** --- No limits
- **TheMealDB** --- Free tier (no key needed for v1)
- **TheCocktailDB** --- Free tier (no key needed for v1)
- **Open Food Facts** --- Fully open data
- **Open Library API** --- Fully open
- **MusicBrainz** --- Fully open
- **JSONPlaceholder** --- Fake data, always free
- **RandomUser.me** --- Always free
- **DiceBear** --- Always free
- **JokeAPI** --- Always free
- **ZenQuotes / Advice Slip** --- Always free
- **ip-api.com** --- Free for non-commercial use

## Free Key Required --- Permanent Free Tier

- **NASA APIs** --- Free forever, generous rate limits
- **TMDB** --- Free forever for non-commercial projects
- **The Guardian API** --- Free developer key, permanent
- **GitHub REST API** --- Free forever (higher limits with token)
