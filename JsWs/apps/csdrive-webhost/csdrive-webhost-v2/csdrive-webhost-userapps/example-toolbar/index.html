This demo shows a small working toolbar a page can give itself: icon buttons that call straight back into the backend, no different from any other button on the page — a back step, suspend, close this tab, close the window outright, and its own × to hide itself — plus the tab's label/tags/root markup the backend renders for you (topBarHtml), inserted as is into #toolbar-info. It starts shown or hidden following topBarHidden, and listens for the admin-app's "Show the top bar" to reappear; closing it again is remembered until that happens.
